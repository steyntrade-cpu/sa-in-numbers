## Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn

The warmest 1 October to 15 November since 1981.

🌡️ 22.5 °C average across South Africa, 2.5 °C above the 1991–2020 average
📈 0.9 °C warmer than the previous record, 2015
📅 37 of 46 days warmer than average for the date

Neither half set a record on its own. October tied 2015 and 2022, and early November was just behind 2004. The record comes from both halves being hot.

What the data can't tell us: these are model values (NASA POWER reanalysis), not thermometer readings; the latest weeks are provisional; and the analysis does not separate El Niño from long-term warming.
