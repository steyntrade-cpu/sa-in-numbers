## Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn

The driest October since 1981 on the Highveld and in Limpopo.

🌧️ Highveld: 9 mm, 12% of the normal 70 mm
🌧️ Limpopo & Lowveld: 6 mm, 12% of the normal 53 mm
🌾 North West & Free State: 30%, the 2nd driest
🌊 Eastern Cape & East Griqualand: 123%, above normal

Since then, storms in early November lifted the Highveld to 77% of normal for the season so far. Dams are 93.5% full.

What the data can't tell us: CHIRPS rainfall is a satellite-and-gauge estimate; at totals this small, the Limpopo ranking (6.4 mm against 8.1 mm in 2005) is within the estimation error. One dry month does not decide a season.
